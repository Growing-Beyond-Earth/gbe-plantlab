# GBE PlantLab

Software, guides, and sample programs for the Growing Beyond Earth PlantLab —
a classroom plant-growth chamber.

## Get the software

Your PlantLab updates itself over WiFi or through the GBE Connect app. You only
need a download here for a **first-time install** (upgrading from the original
MicroPython software) or to **recover** a unit.

**[Download the latest PlantLab software](https://growingbeyond.earth/update.php)**

Then follow the [installation guide](FLASHING.md) — it takes about two
minutes and needs nothing but a USB cable.

## Learn to program your PlantLab

Your PlantLab follows a schedule you write yourself — lights, fan, watering,
even experiments that react to sensors.

- **[Programming Your PlantLab](docs/PROGRAMMING_YOUR_PLANTLAB.md)** — start
  here: lights, fan, and timing.
- **[Advanced PlantLab Programming](docs/ADVANCED_PLANTLAB_PROGRAMMING.md)** —
  watering schedules, sensors, multi-day experiments, sunrise effects.
- **[PlantLab Programming Dictionary](docs/PLANTLAB_PROGRAMMING_DICTIONARY.md)**
  — every setting, in one place.
- **[Chatbot reference](docs/PLANTLAB_CHATBOT_REFERENCE.md)** — upload this
  file to an AI assistant and ask it to help write your program.
- **[What's New](docs/WHATS_NEW_FOR_TEACHERS.md)** — upgrading from the
  original MicroPython software? Read this first.

## Sample programs

The [`samples/`](samples/) folder holds ready-to-use `program.json` files —
copy one onto your SD card, rename it to `program.json`, and adapt it to your
class. Start with `classroom_starter.json`.
